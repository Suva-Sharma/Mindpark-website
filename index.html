At MindPark, our mission is to empower children with ADHD and Autism through engaging and innovative learning activities and games.

Some Games We Have

Color Clash

In this game, we select the color of the word that appears on the screen.

Voiceloon

In this game, we blow on the phone to make the balloon go high.

Number Counting

In this game, we count the number of items present on the screen and select the right number option.

Benefits

Enhances Cognitive Skills

Stimulates critical thinking and problem-solving, developing strong analytical abilities through puzzles and symbol identification.

Improves Focus and Attention

Boosts attention span by engaging children in fast-paced, interactive games.

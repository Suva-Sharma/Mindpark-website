AutismCare Nepal Society

"A society where PWA enjoy independent and dignified life."

AutismCare Nepal Society (ACNS) was founded on 2nd April 2008 on the occasion of the World Autism Awareness Day. It is the only active autism organization in Nepal that is run by passionate parents that care for persons with autism. It is a non-governmental, nonprofit making, non-political NGO registered in District Administration Office (Regd. No. 1067/2065) and affiliated to Social Welfare Council (SWC No: 25314).

We respect and value the uniqueness of all individuals within the autism spectrum; with appropriate interventions, each person can make some contribution to his/her family, community and to society.

We demonstrate respect by advancing an individualized, person-centered approach that is based upon the person's distinctive strengths, abilities, interests, and choices; the person with autism is the priority for us. We cherish, value and appreciate the untiring efforts our staff, caregivers, parents to support and better the lives and well-being of all PWA.
